About

Before support I worked in hospitals, first as a patient care assistant on a behavioral health unit, then registering patients in an emergency department. That's documentation where accuracy, protocol, and deadlines matter, with people who are often having a very bad day.

It turned out to be the same work in a different building. From IT service desks to a COVID-19 testing company to a domain registrar, the thread has stayed the same: get the details right, and write them down so the next person can too.

Today I split my time between the support queue and the layer underneath it: the documentation and terminology people and AI both rely on, and the AI rules and tests that keep it accurate.
