About me

I didn't take a straight line into tech, and I'm glad. Before support I worked in hospitals, first as a patient care assistant on a behavioral health unit, then registering patients in an emergency department. That's documentation where accuracy, protocol, and deadlines matter, with people who are often having a very bad day.

It turned out to be the same work in a different building. From IT service desks to a COVID-19 testing company to a domain registrar, the thread has stayed the same: get the details right, write them down so the next person can too, and stay kind while you do it.

Today I split my time between the support queue and the layer underneath it: the documentation, terminology, and AI rules that everyone, human or machine, answers from.
