# Services and prices

**Stephanie Coulter · Remote, US and Canada · Prices in USD**

I make sure customers, and the AI answering them, can find the right answer, in technical products where a wrong answer costs something. There are two ways to work with me: help center and AI projects at a fixed price, and support hours by the hour.

---

## Start here: the AI answer check

**$600, fixed. Five business days.**

Your AI assistant answers customers from your help center. Is it right? I ask your assistant and your help center search 25 questions your customers actually ask, each twice, because AI answers change from run to run. Every answer is scored: right, partly right, wrong, or made up.

You get the scored answers, a two-page report on the worst wrong answers and why each happened, and the three fixes that matter most. You send the questions from your tickets, with customer details removed, or I write them from your docs and forum. No access to your systems is needed.

No AI assistant yet? The same check runs against your help center search, at the same price. The full $600 is credited toward a bigger project booked within 60 days.
